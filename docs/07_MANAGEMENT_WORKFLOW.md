# Management Workflow

The reports are decision tools, not a requirement to read every page in sequence. The internal and competitive reports contain **173 pages in total**, including detailed evidence and reference appendices. Those appendices support questions and follow-up; they do not all need to be presented during the meeting.

## Recommended Workflow

1. Present the internal Al Assiel report.
2. Present the competitive report for market context.
3. Select 3–5 priority actions.
4. Assign one responsible owner to each action.
5. Define a deadline.
6. Define a measurable KPI.
7. Implement the actions.
8. Monitor for approximately 30 days.
9. Plan a deliberate review refresh, using an incremental or bounded newest-first process where supported.
10. Rerun the analysis against the updated stored reviews.
11. Compare the new period with the pre-action baseline.

## Suggested Meeting Output

For every selected action, record:

| Decision field | Example question |
| --- | --- |
| Action | What exactly will change? |
| Evidence | Which recurring customer signal supports it? |
| Owner | Who is accountable? |
| Deadline | By when will it be implemented? |
| KPI | What will show improvement? |
| Review date | When will management check progress? |

The objective is a short owned action list, not another redesign of the reports.

## Evidence and Optional Research

Use report appendices when management wants to inspect review cases, methodology, time windows, menu evidence, or detailed comparisons.

Competitor physical visits are optional. They can add first-hand evidence about dishes, presentation, portions, service, drinks, buffet, and offers, but they are not required to begin implementing the evidence-backed actions from Phase 1.

Use the [Final Management Package](../final_manager_package/) for the meeting and [How to Run](03_HOW_TO_RUN.md) when it is time to refresh the analysis.
